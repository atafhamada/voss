# Governance

## Model

VOSS is currently maintained by a single maintainer (BDFL model):
**Ataf Hamada** (https://github.com/atafhamada).

## Decisions

- Design decisions are documented in `DESIGN.md`.
- Changes to the public API or scope require an update to `DESIGN.md` first.
- The maintainer has final say on all decisions.

## Contributions

Community contributions are welcome (see `CONTRIBUTING.md`).
All merged contributions become part of VOSS under BSL-1.1.

## Future

As the project grows, this document will be updated to reflect a broader
governance structure.
